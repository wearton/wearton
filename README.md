# Hi, I build Wearton 👋

Wearton is a personal AI stylist. It learns your colours from one selfie: your colour season (1 of 12), your undertone and a palette of about 8 colours. It keeps your clothes in a digital closet and tells you what to wear today, for the weather and your plans.

### 🚀 Projects
- [Wearton](https://wearton.app/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=profile&utm_campaign=launch): AI stylist and digital wardrobe. Colour analysis from one selfie, a digital closet, today's outfit for the weather, Hooky the stylist chat and virtual try-on.

Wearton works on iPhone and Android, on the web at [my.wearton.app](https://my.wearton.app), as a browser extension for [Chrome](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/wearton/ngooiemonmmdnjedjioonghmbmgbfaie), [Edge](https://microsoftedge.microsoft.com/addons/detail/wearton-digital-wardrobe/inpolamfocdbimfcjmdmpchncbfmekad), [Firefox](https://addons.mozilla.org/firefox/addon/wearton-digital-wardrobe/) and Safari, and in ChatGPT and Claude. It is available in 12 languages: English, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Polish, Portuguese, Russian, Turkish, Ukrainian, Chinese and Japanese.
